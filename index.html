Ground sample distance, m/px
Ground sample distance is how many metres of ground one pixel covers — roughly altitude × sensor pitch ÷ focal length. A DJI-class 1/2.3″ camera at 20 m is about 0.008. Without it we cannot confirm your image sits inside the resolution range the model was validated on, and the result is flagged accordingly.

Field boundary — recommended

Click points around your field, then Close boundary. Everything outside stops counting. This is the only reliable way to exclude a pond — muddy water and wet soil are almost the same colour, so no automatic rule separates them. Without a boundary, neighbouring plots, roads and water enter your field's numbers.

      One HTTP call per image. No SDK, no model to host, no GPU. Returns the
      vigour map, canopy cover, management zones and a full coverage audit as
      JSON.

      
curl -X POST https://nabhya-api-5mpnb7wq4q-el.a.run.app/v1/ndvi \
  -H "X-API-Key: YOUR_KEY" \
  -F "image=@field.jpg" \
  -F "gsd_m_per_px=0.008" \
  -F 'aoi_polygon=[[0.1,0.2],[0.8,0.2],[0.8,0.9],[0.1,0.9]]'
